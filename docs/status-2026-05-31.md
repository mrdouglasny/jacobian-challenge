## Validating the definitions and axioms

Closing the sorries via definitions + axioms raises a separate question
from "are the proofs done": **are the definitions and axioms the right
ones?** This is verified mechanically where possible —
[`axiom-report.txt`](axiom-report.txt) (golden `#print axioms` trace,
regenerated by [`scripts/axiom_report.lean`](../scripts/axiom_report.lean))
confirms no `sorryAx` hides under any closed declaration, and that
`genus_Elliptic_eq_one` needs **no project axioms** — and is laid out as a
plan in [`validation-plan.md`](validation-plan.md): the definition-vs-axiom
asymmetry, an axiom taxonomy by validation risk, a prioritized backlog
(CI axiom guard → discharge an anti-hack lemma on a concrete curve →
non-vacuity sentinels → human review of classical axioms), and a
human-readable object-contract format plus an AI-modelable
specification-first pipeline.
