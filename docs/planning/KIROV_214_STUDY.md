## 1. The rank-2g mechanism (Q1)

### 1.1 The design move that removes H₁

```
truePeriodLattice X : Submodule ℤ (Fin (genus X) → ℂ)
  := Submodule.span ℤ (closedLoopPeriods X)        -- PeriodLattice.lean:77-80
```

The lattice is the ℤ-span of the period vectors of **all** closed smooth
loops (`closedLoopPeriods X` = image of `periodVec` over
`IsClosedSmoothLoop`, PeriodLattice.lean:30). There is no finite generating
family, no `H1`, no Hurewicz map anywhere in the lattice chain. Genus is
analytic by definition: `genus X := Module.finrank ℂ (HolomorphicOneForms
X)` (Genus.lean:66-68). Forster 21.4's "`H₁ → H⁰(Ω)*` has discrete
full-rank image" is replaced by: *the subgroup of ℂ^g generated by all loop
periods is discrete and ℝ-spanning*; Mathlib's ZLattice theory then forces
it to be free of rank `finrank ℝ (ℂ^g) = 2g`. The number `2g` enters the
development at exactly ONE place:

```
hrank : finrank ℤ (truePeriodLattice X) = 2 * genus X
  := by rw [ZLattice.rank ℝ, finrank_real_of_complex,
            Module.finrank_pi, Fintype.card_fin]   -- PeriodLatticeBasis.lean:74-75
```

i.e. `2g = dim_ℝ ℂ^g`, pure linear algebra. Two paragraphs of Mathlib then
produce the headline (statement frozen verbatim from the dissection era):

* `instance : DiscreteTopology (truePeriodLattice X)` —
  PeriodLatticeBasis.lean:38-53, from the isolated-zero window (1.4 below)
  via `discreteTopology_iff_isOpen_singleton_zero` (a subgroup is discrete
  iff `0` is isolated).
* `instance : IsZLattice ℝ (truePeriodLattice X)` —
  PeriodLatticeBasis.lean:57-58, one line from the spanning theorem (1.3).
* `exists_periodLattice_realBasis : ∃ b : Basis (Fin (2 * genus X)) ℝ
  (Fin (genus X) → ℂ), truePeriodLattice X = span ℤ (range ⇑b)` —
  PeriodLatticeBasis.lean:66-82, via `ZLattice.module_free`,
  `ZLattice.module_finite`, the `hrank` rewrite above, reindexing the
  `chooseBasis`, and `Basis.ofZLatticeBasis` + `ofZLatticeBasis_span`.

So "his Jacobian is compact" is downstream bookkeeping: ℂ^g modulo the
ℤ-span of an ℝ-basis. The actual mathematics is the
discreteness/non-degeneracy pair, proven by the four lemmas below.

### 1.2 B-1 — Forster 21.3: g base points with invertible evaluation matrix

* `formEvalSelf a : HolomorphicOneForms X →ₗ[ℂ] ℂ` (JacobiBasePoints.lean:40)
  — the chart-centre coefficient functional `α ↦ localRep α a a`.
* `exists_finset_formEvalSelf_ker (k ≤ g) : ∃ s : Finset X, s.card = k ∧
  finrank ℂ (⨅ a ∈ s, ker (formEvalSelf a)) = g − k`
  (JacobiBasePoints.lean:83-116). *Proof idea:* induction on `k`; base case
  is `finrank H⁰(Ω) = g` (definitional). At each step the current kernel
  `V` has `dim = g − n > 0`; pick `0 ≠ α ∈ V`; the identity-theorem atom
  `exists_localRep_self_ne_zero` (Dolbeault/FormCoeff.lean:77 — a nonzero
  form has a nonzero coefficient at SOME chart centre) provides a new point
  `a'` where `α` doesn't vanish; intersecting with `ker (formEvalSelf a')`
  drops the dimension by exactly one.
* `jacobiEvalMatrix a : Matrix (Fin g) (Fin g) ℂ`, `A i j = localRep
  (periodBasisForm X i) (a j) (a j)` (JacobiBasePoints.lean:150-152).
* `jacobiEvalMatrix_det_ne_zero` (JacobiBasePoints.lean:158-191): a nonzero
  left null vector `v` would make `α = ∑ v i • ωᵢ` a nonzero form vanishing
  at every `a j`, contradicting the 21.3 property. Packaged as
  `exists_jacobiBasePoints_det_ne_zero` (JacobiBasePoints.lean:195-200).

### 1.3 B-3 — Forster 21.4(c): the lattice ℝ-spans ℂ^g (non-degeneracy)

`span_real_truePeriodLattice_eq_top`
(PeriodLatticeNondegenerate.lean:108-296). *Proof idea (5 lines):* if the
span were proper, a dual functional kills it; every ℝ-functional on ℂ^g is
`v ↦ Re ∑ dⱼvⱼ` (`exists_re_dotProduct_repr`, :64-98). Set `u(Q) := Re ∑
dⱼ · periodVec(smoothPath x₀ Q)ⱼ` — well-defined as a bare function;
near any `Q₀` it equals `Re ∘ H_{Q₀} ∘ chart` with `H_{Q₀}` the analytic
chart primitive `∑ dⱼ Φ̃_{Q₀,j}`, **because two paths with the same
endpoints differ by a closed loop, whose period the functional kills** —
this is `localLift_quotient_eq_ofCurve_eventually`
(OfCurveAnalyticitySkeleton.lean:1322, used at :164), and it is precisely
where "lattice = ALL loop periods" pays: lattice-membership of the
correction loop is `periodVec_mem_truePeriodLattice_of_closed`
(PeriodLattice.lean:85-88), i.e. *definitional*. At a maximum of `u`
(compactness), the planar open-mapping dichotomy
(`AnalyticAt.eventually_constant_or_nhds_le_map_nhds`, packaged as
`eventually_const_of_re_le`, :40-58) forces local constancy of `H`; the
max level set is clopen (:223-236), so `u` is constant; differentiating
each chart model at its centre kills every coefficient of `∑ dⱼωⱼ`
(:238-268); the identity theorem (FormCoeff.lean:77, used at :288) gives
`d = 0`. **No harmonic theory, no surface integration** — the only
analysis atom is Mathlib's open-mapping dichotomy.

### 1.4 B-2 + B-4 — Forster 21.4(a)+(b): discreteness via the Abel engine and residues

**B-2 (the IFT window).** `jacobiMap a z i := ∑ⱼ Φ̃_{a j, i}(z j)`
(JacobiLocalMap.lean:119-121) — the local Jacobi map at the B-1 base
points, each coordinate read in its own chart. Its strict Fréchet
derivative at the centre is assembled by hand from one-variable strict
derivatives of the chart primitives (`jacobiMap_hasStrictFDerivAt`, :152-179)
and equals `jacobiEvalMatrix a` acting as a linear map; `det ≠ 0` upgrades
it to a continuous linear equivalence, and Mathlib's
`HasStrictFDerivAt.map_nhds_eq_of_equiv` gives openness:
`jacobiMap_map_nhds : map G (𝓝 center) = 𝓝 0` (:205-214); packaged with
B-1 as `exists_jacobiMap_map_nhds` (:219-227). No manifold IFT.

**B-4 (isolated zero).** `truePeriodLattice_isolated_zero : ∃ U ∈ 𝓝 0,
∀ v ∈ Γ, v ∈ U → v = 0` (PeriodLatticeDiscrete.lean:120-557). *Proof
sketch:*

1. Take the window `U = G '' V`, `V` a polydisc of chart balls shrunk into
   pairwise-disjoint T2 neighborhoods `O j ∋ a j`
   (`exists_pairwise_disjoint_opens`, :87-115; window at :143-148).
2. Suppose `0 ≠ t ∈ Γ ∩ U`, so `t = G(z)`. Set `x j := chart⁻¹(z j) ∈ O j`;
   some `x j₀ ≠ a j₀` (:177-187).
3. Build the 1-chain `c` = straight chart segments `a j → x j` MINUS the
   finite ℤ-combination of closed loops realizing `t ∈ span ℤ
   (closedLoopPeriods)` (`Submodule.mem_span_set'` at :229-231; chain at
   :233-245). Its boundary is `∑ⱼ (xⱼ − aⱼ)` (:247-272); its basis periods
   all vanish (:349-394) because segment values sum to `G(z)ᵢ = tᵢ` (the
   FTC-on-a-ball read `hseg_val`, :274-347) and the loop part contributes
   `−tᵢ`.
4. **The engine:** `exists_meromorphic_of_oneChain`
   (AbelEngineMeromorphic.lean:72-78) — Forster 20.7 sufficiency — returns
   meromorphic `f` with `div f = ∂c` AND the centred local normal form
   `f̂ = H·(w − w₀)^{∂c(a)}` with `H` analytic, `H(w₀) ≠ 0`, at EVERY point
   (the Laurent data the residue read needs). Constructed as
   `exp(−u) · G` from the ∂̄-potential of the chain
   (AbelEngineMeromorphic.lean:83-110).
5. **The residue theorem** on each `f·ωᵢ`:
   `SerreResidueTheorem.residueTheorem_unconditional`
   (Dolbeault/SerreResidueRamifiedRealSlitGeometry.lean:1017-1022), applied
   at :469-474. The residue at a simple pole `a j` is `H(w₀)·ωᵢ-coefficient`
   = `cvec j · A i j` via the `dslope`-split atom
   `resAt_analyticAt_mul_sub_inv` (:45-74, applied :476-521); residues
   vanish at the `x j` (divisor coefficient ≥ 0, :522-532).
6. Assemble `A.mulVec cvec = 0` with `cvec ≠ 0` (the leading coefficient at
   `a j₀` is nonzero by the engine's normal form, :546-551) — contradiction
   with B-1's `det A ≠ 0` (:552-557).

**Answer to Q1 in one sentence:** nothing "replaces H₁" — the period map
from H₁ is replaced by the *definition* `Γ := span ℤ (all loop periods)`;
discreteness of that a-priori-infinitely-generated subgroup is proven
directly (every nonzero lattice vector in the IFT window would bound a
principal divisor contradicting `det A ≠ 0`), and `rank = 2g` then falls
out of `ZLattice.rank` rather than being fed in from a Betti-number count.

## 2. R1/R2: does his lattice need the bilinear relations? (Q2)

**No — at all.** The retirement note (CutSurfaceRelations.lean:151-158)
is explicit: the cut-surface `exists_cutSurface` sorry and its derived
`exists_canonicalDissection` "are GONE: `exists_periodLattice_realBasis` is
now proven via Forster 21.4 …, which never needs a dissection"; the proven
`cutSurface_R1` (:127-131, box-Cauchy) and `cutSurface_R2` (:136-138,
Green positivity) "remain banked as hypothesis-conditional archival
theorems". Neither `PeriodLatticeDiscrete.lean` nor
`PeriodLatticeNondegenerate.lean` nor `PeriodLatticeBasis.lean` imports
them. Per `KIROV_ROUTE_IDEAS.md` §5 his verdict is that R1/R2 are "not
needed by the challenge" once the lattice comes from 21.4; the symplectic
basis is "a consequence, not an input" (Forster 21.5 Remark).

Mechanism comparison against our two discreteness proofs:

| | needs positivity (R2)? | needs generation (2g count)? | analytic engine consumed |
|---|---|---|---|
| OURS #214 `discreteTopology_familyPeriodLattice_of_arcBoundaryWordData` (OURS: `RiemannSurface/PeriodDiscretenessFromR2.lean:502`) | **yes** — Siegel: R2 makes the A-block invertible, `Im τ ≻ 0` makes the `[I∣τ]` columns ℝ-independent | yes for the FULL lattice — `PeriodGeneratingLoops` collapses all loop periods onto the 2g family (`:522-540`) | the port's boundary-word R1/R2 engines (Green positivity) over `ArcBoundaryWordData` |

The trade he makes: the Hodge-positivity wall (our R2 / `Im τ ≻ 0`) and
the topological-generation wall (our T-FG/T-RANK feeding
`PeriodGeneratingLoops`) are BOTH bypassed, at the price of consuming the
∂̄-machinery (the Abel engine rests on `h¹(𝒪) = g` + Forster 19.10
∂̄-solvability) and the residue theorem. For him that machinery existed
already (built for Abel's theorem); **for us it also already exists** —
see §4. Note the pleasing inversion: in the classical narrative R2 is what
makes the period torus *polarized*; for mere lattice-ness/compactness
Forster 21.4 shows residues + maximum principle suffice.
